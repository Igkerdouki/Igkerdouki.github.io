How I built it

A toy, not a test

3D in the browser

Built with three.js: the girls, the gym, the rink and every flip and fall are animated in real time.

No words, no menus

Designed for kids who can't read yet. Everything is a tap on a picture: a girl, an outfit, a trick.

Falling is the fun

Nothing can be done wrong. A fall ends in a honk or a squeaky scream, so the mistake becomes the joke.

From web to iPhone

The same code ships as a native iOS app through Capacitor, on its way to the App Store.

For grown-ups

No ads. No scores. No pressure. Patita is a toy, not a test — falling over is the best part.
